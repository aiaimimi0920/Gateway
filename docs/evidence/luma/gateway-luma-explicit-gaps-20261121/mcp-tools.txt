Tool list
Every tool the Luma connector gives your assistant, and what each one does.
For developers and admins. Your assistant chooses these tools for you; you don’t need to call them by name.
Tool
What it does
luma_session_open
Opens a workspace session. Reuse a workspace key to keep assets between chats.
luma_session_send_brief
Sends a free-text request to Luma’s creative agent.
luma_session_run_capability
Runs a pre-set workflow (an app) with its form fields.
luma_session_await
Waits for progress, questions and results on a session.
luma_run_respond
Answers a question or review a workflow is waiting on.
luma_generate_image
,
luma_generate_video
,
luma_generate_layering
One model call, one result.
luma_run_await
Waits for a direct generation to finish.
luma_session_cancel
Stops one run or the whole session. Outputs already made are kept.
luma_workspace_upload_widget
,
luma_workspace_upload_asset
,
luma_workspace_complete_upload
Gets your files into the workspace.
luma_workspace_list_assets
,
luma_workspace_view_asset
,
luma_workspace_list
Lists, shows and finds workspace files and workspaces.
luma_library_*
Reads and organizes your team’s Asset Library.
luma_capability_list
,
luma_capability_describe
Lists the workflows you can run and their form fields.
luma_model_list
,
luma_model_describe
Lists the image and video models and their options.
luma_usage
Credits used and left, per team, session or asset.
Every tool that starts work takes an
idempotency_key
, so a retry never makes duplicate work. Developers building on the same functionality should read
MCP and the API
.
