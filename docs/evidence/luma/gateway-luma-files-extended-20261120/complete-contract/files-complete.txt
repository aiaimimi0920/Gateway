Complete a presigned upload
POST
/files/{file_id}/complete
Finalize a presigned upload after you have PUT the bytes to the upload URL. Kicks off ingest/moderation and returns the file, which transitions to
ready
(or
failed
) asynchronously — poll GET /files/{file_id} to observe the terminal state.
