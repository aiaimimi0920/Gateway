Usage and credits
Read your credit balance and what you spent with GET /v1/credits and GET /v1/usage, and convert credits to dollars.
The API charges in
credits
. Two endpoints tell you where they went:
GET /v1/credits
for what is left, and
GET /v1/usage
for what you spent. Both take your API key, the same one you generate with.

What one generation cost
Section titled “What one generation cost”
GET /v1/generations/{generation_id}
reports what that one generation was actually charged, in
usage.credits_charged
. It is the charge billing posted: not an estimate, not the credits held while the generation ran, and not a price from
Pricing
.
Terminal window
curl
-s
"https://agents.lumalabs.ai/v1/generations/d290f1ee-6c54-4b01-90e6-d701748f0851"
\
-H
"Authorization: Bearer
$LUMA_API_KEY
"
The
usage
field of that response reads one of four ways:
usage
Means
{"credits_charged": 14}
Billing settled the generation and charged 14 credits.
{"credits_charged": 0}
Billing settled the generation and charged nothing, for example a failure.
{"credits_charged": null}
Billing has not settled the generation yet, or could not confirm its charge.
null
Usage reporting is not switched on yet.
Billing settles separately from the generation.
A generation can reach
completed
or
failed
before its charge is settled, so
credits_charged
can still be
null
on a finished generation. Keep polling the same endpoint after the generation finishes, at a modest interval that grows between attempts, and stop at a cutoff you choose. There is no guaranteed time for the value to arrive.
0
means billing settled the generation and charged nothing.
Like any number here, it reflects what was posted when you read it.
null
is never a zero.
Some generations stay
null
: ones settled before this field existed that were not charged, and ones whose charge billing could not confirm. If a value is still
null
at your cutoff, record it as unknown rather than as free.
A generation newly created by
POST /v1/generations
reports
credits_charged: null
, since nothing has been charged yet. When
POST
returns a generation that already exists instead of creating one, its
usage
is what
GET
would return.
usage
itself is
null
while usage reporting is switched off. It is switched on for every API client at once, and polling does not change it.
To total charges across many generations, use
What you spent
instead of adding these up.
Credits and dollars
Section titled “Credits and dollars”
Every figure the API returns is in credits. To convert, multiply by your rate in
USD per credit
:
If
GET /v1/usage
returns a non-null
overage_rate
, that is your rate
for credits past your allowance. It is a decimal string; parse it as a decimal, not a float, if you bill on it.
Otherwise, your plan or contract sets your rate.
If you do not know it, ask your Luma contact. Do not assume a default rate.
dollars = credits × rate
.
Pricing
quotes a price per generation; do not divide it by a generation’s credits to get a rate, because credits are whole numbers and the division does not come out to your rate.
